# Goals and Curatorial Principles

## Primary Goal

Create a durable, explorable time capsule of the scaling era from the perspective of someone living through it. The archive should help a future visitor understand not only what changed, but how the change felt while its outcome was still uncertain.

## What Belongs in Feel The AGI

The folder is deliberately broader than direct AI announcements. Relevant material includes:

- AGI, frontier models, agents, and changing software work
- Robotics, autonomous systems, drones, and surveillance
- Medical and scientific breakthroughs
- China's technological rise and changing geopolitical capacity
- Infrastructure, energy, manufacturing, and state capacity
- Labor, inequality, cost of living, and late-stage capitalism
- Prediction markets and bleakly financialized views of the future
- Everyday normalization, humor, anxiety, awe, and generational change

The strongest artifacts often look ordinary. A person asking ChatGPT where pasta is located, an AI agent obtaining a prescription, or a joke about forgetting how to code can describe the era more vividly than a benchmark table.

## Other Bookmark Buckets

The original 3,189 bookmarks were manually assigned to one primary bucket:

- **Feel The AGI**: 1,483 entries used by this site
- **For Later Reference**: 874 practical resources and technical references
- **Inspiration**: 832 creative, aesthetic, cultural, and personal artifacts

## Product Goals

- Preserve source attribution and link to the original post.
- Default to reverse chronology, because the pace and sequence of change matter.
- Support random ordering so distant moments can collide and reveal themes.
- Resemble X enough to preserve the original reading context without impersonating the live service.
- Remain useful on mobile, where the feed should feel natural and media should be legible.
- Keep the first page fast even as the collection grows.
- Be explicit about missing or incomplete media instead of faking fidelity.

## Non-Goals

- Recreating X interactions such as posting, liking, or reposting
- Reporting live engagement counts
- Automatically deciding what is historically important
- Treating every AI-related tweet as time-capsule material
- Replacing the original source post
